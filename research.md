1. <details markdown="1">
   <summary markdown="span">Pair correlation of $L$-zeros and Hooley's conjecture. Available upon request.</summary>

   **Abstract.** In this article, we study the form factor for zeros of Dirichlet $L$-functions, pairing zeros within each primitive character and then averaging over primitive characters modulo a prime conductor. Unconditionally, we show for $\vert\alpha\vert<1$ that the form factor agrees with the random matrix prediction from the Circular Unitary Ensemble (CUE). Under GRH and the Hardy--Littlewood twin primes conjecture, we extend this agreement to $\vert\alpha\vert<2$. For $\vert\alpha\vert\ge1$, we prove that the CUE prediction is equivalent to Hooley's conjecture for the variance of primes in arithmetic progressions with prime moduli. We also explore applications of these form-factor results to the simplicity of zeros.

   </details>
2. <details markdown="1">
   <summary markdown="span">Explicit quadratic large sieve inequality. [_Acta Arithmetica,_ **223(3)**:227-252](https://doi.org/10.4064/aa250722-27-1) (2026).</summary>

   **Abstract.** We obtain an explicit version of Heath-Brown’s large sieve inequality for quadratic characters and discuss its applications to $L$-functions and quadratic fields.

   </details>
3. <details markdown="1">
   <summary markdown="span">On sums of Fourier coefficients of cusp forms twisted with additive characters (2023). [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.00006).</summary>

   **Abstract.** When $a_n$ is the $n$'th coefficient of some holomorphic cusp form, we prove a variety of Omega results for the twisted sum $\sum_{n\le x}a_ne^{2\pi i n\alpha}$ and discuss their applications to the Ramanujan $\tau$-function and sums of $a_n$ over arithmetic progressions.

   </details>
4. <details markdown="1">
   <summary markdown="span">On a weighted sum over multiplicative functions and its applications to the GPY sieve (2022). [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.01671).</summary>

   **Abstract.** In this paper, we investigate the asymptotics of a class of weighted sums over multiplicative functions and apply our results to deduce a stronger asymptotic form of Yitang Zhang's smoothened GPY sieve with coefficient expressions that are friendly to numerical computations.

   </details>
5. <details markdown="1">
   <summary markdown="span">A corrected simplified proof of Chen’s theorem (2022). [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2203.07871).</summary>

   **Abstract.** In 1973, J.-R. Chen showed that every large even integer is a sum of a prime and a product of at most two primes. In this paper, the author indicates and fixes the issues in a simplified proof of this result given by Pan, Wang, and Ding.

   </details>

6. <details markdown="1">
   <summary markdown="span">PM2.5 Density Prediction Based on a Two-Stage Rolling Forecast Model Using LightGBM. In W. Cao, A. Ozcan, H. Xie, & B. Guan (Eds.), [_Computing and Data Science (Vol. 1513, pp. 228–248)_](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-16-8885-0_19) (2021). Springer Nature Singapore.</summary>

   **Abstract.** At present, air pollution is a primary issue of the world. Particularly, PM2.5 pollution can cause severe impact on economy and human health, so developing an accurate PM2.5 prediction model becomes a hot topic. Up to now, researchers had developed PM2.5 forecasting methods based on decision tree models, RNN models, and hybrid models. Previous works also discovered plentiful features, such as seasonal data and weather forecasting data that help increase the accuracy of PM2.5 prediction. To improve the model accuracy, we developed a LightGBM-based PM2.5 prediction model that has two innovations: 1) our model studies how special events (e.g., diplomatic visits, sport events, and government meetings) influence PM2.5 variation. 2) our model adopts the strategy of two-stage rolling forecasting so that it can achieve high accuracy without relying on weather forecasting data.

   </details>
